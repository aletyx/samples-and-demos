# Theme

The app uses the [Gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox) dark palette,
soft contrast ([theme.dart](../mortgagehub_app/lib/theme.dart)), the same as
Presence's: the page is `bg0_s` (`#32302F`), panels and cards step up
through `bg1` and `bg2`, text is `fg` (`#EBDBB2`), and the primary color is
yellow (`#FABD2F`), secondary aqua and tertiary blue. App bars are flat and
the page's color, with the title bold and on the left.
